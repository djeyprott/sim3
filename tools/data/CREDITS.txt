sim3 world data: sources and credits

Terrain today
  NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (2022). ETOPO 2022
  15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model (used at 60 arc-seconds).
  https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74

Ice-age topography and ice extent
  Peltier, W.R., Argus, D.F. and Drummond, R. (2015). Space geodesy
  constrains ice age terminal deglaciation: The global ICE-6G_C (VM5a)
  model. J. Geophys. Res. Solid Earth, 120, 450-487.
  https://doi.org/10.1002/2014JB011176
  Argus, D.F., Peltier, W.R., Drummond, R. and Moore, A.W. (2014). The
  Antarctica component of postglacial rebound model ICE-6G_C (VM5a) based
  on GPS positioning, exposure age dating of ice thicknesses, and relative
  sea level histories. Geophys. J. Int., 198, 537-563.
  Distributed by PMIP4: https://pmip4.lsce.ipsl.fr/doku.php/data:ice_ice6g_c

Observed temperatures and precipitation 1981-2010
  Karger, D.N. et al. (2017). Climatologies at high resolution for the
  earth's land surface areas. Scientific Data 4, 170122. CHELSA V2.1:
  https://doi.org/10.16904/envidat.228.v2.1

Earth's orbit
  Berger, A. and Loutre, M.F. (1991). Insolation values for the climate of
  the last 10 million years. Quaternary Science Reviews 10, 297-317.
  Table via NOAA NCEI Paleoclimatology.

CO2
  Bereiter, B. et al. (2015). Revision of the EPICA Dome C CO2 record from
  800 to 600 kyr before present. Geophys. Res. Lett. 42, 542-549
  (Antarctic composite). NOAA NCEI Paleoclimatology.
  Mauna Loa annual mean CO2: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory.

Volcanic eruptions
  HolVol v1.1: Sigl, M. & Toohey, M. (2024), PANGAEA,
  https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.971971 (CC BY 4.0); method in Sigl et al.
  (2022), Earth Syst. Sci. Data 14, 3167-3196. Only the sizes of eruptions
  between 9500 and 500 BCE are used, to set how often and how big sim3's own
  random eruptions are.

Processing
  Aggregated onto sim3's geodesic grid by tools/data/build_world.py in
  https://github.com/djeyprott/sim3 (GPL-3.0-or-later). Ice thickness in
  10,000 BCE is derived from ICE-6G surface heights by isostatic balance; it
  is sim3's estimate, not part of ICE-6G.
